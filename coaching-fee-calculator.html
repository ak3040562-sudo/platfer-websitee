Common questions

Does this connect to my bank or student database?

No. This is a standalone calculator and does not connect to banking or Platfer accounts.

Does it calculate different fee plans per student?

This version assumes one monthly fee for all students. For mixed fee plans, calculate each batch separately and add the totals.

Page-specific guide

How to estimate coaching fee collections

A fee collection estimate helps an institute plan expected income from enrolled students. It is a planning figure, not proof that every fee has been paid. Track the amount due, amount received, date and any approved concession separately.

Example: 40 students paying ₹1,000 per month gives a scheduled monthly total of ₹40,000 before discounts, unpaid fees, refunds or other costs. Compare expected and received amounts at a fixed time each month, and share clear receipts or reminders with families.

Keep financial records private and reconcile the total with actual transactions.

Check your institute’s own refund, concession and payment terms before sending reminders.
