Cancelling

How to cancel a subscription

Last updated: October 2026. This page explains our policy in plain language. If you have questions, write to support@platfer.in.

Student subscriptions are billed by Google Play. You can cancel anytime from the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

Refunds

How refunds work

  • Because billing runs through Google Play, refund requests follow Google Play’s refund policy and are decided by Google.
  • If you were charged by mistake (for example a duplicate charge), first check your Google Play order history, then contact us at support@platfer.in with the order ID — we will help you raise it correctly.
  • Free Batch Code joins involve no payment, so there is nothing to refund.
Coaching fees

Fees paid to a coaching institute

Fees you pay directly to a coaching institute (offline or through their own link) are between you and that institute. Platfer cannot refund those, but we can share guidance on how to contact the institute.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Platfer subscription?
Open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions, select Platfer and cancel. Access continues till the paid period ends.
I was charged twice. What should I do?
Check your Google Play order history for duplicate orders, then email support@platfer.in with the order IDs and we will help.
Can I get a refund for a fee paid to my coaching?
Fees paid directly to an institute are handled by that institute. Contact them first; we can guide you on reaching them.
