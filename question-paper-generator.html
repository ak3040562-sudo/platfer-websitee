Question paper generator for teachers & schools

Prepare a simple printable question paper for school exams, tuition classes, chapter tests and coaching mock tests. Add your own questions and marks; the tool totals the marks automatically.

How to use it

  1. Enter class, subject, exam time and instructions.
  2. Add or remove questions and set each question’s marks.
  3. Check the total marks in the preview.
  4. Print or choose Save as PDF.
For official school or institute records, verify the result and follow your institution's rules.

Common questions

Does it automatically create questions from a chapter?

No. This version lets teachers enter their own questions; it does not invent or verify curriculum questions.

Can I save the paper online?

This version does not upload or save the paper to an account. Use Print → Save as PDF to keep a copy.

Frequently asked questions

Achhe question paper ki pehchan kya hai?

Santulan: aasan, madhyam aur kathin prashno ka sahi mishran, poora syllabus cover, aur uttar-kunji (answer key) pehle se taiyaar. Paper set karna hi aadhi pareeksha hai.

Kya online test paper test ki jagah le sakta hai?

Abhyaas aur nidan (diagnosis) ke liye haan. Board-pattern ke anubhav ke liye kabhi-kabhi kagaz wala test bhi rakho — dono ka jod sabse majboot hai.

Har hafte test ke liye paper kaise bane?

Pichhle paper ki galtiyon aur kamzor topics se agla paper banao. Platfer ke test records batate hain ki batch kahan atka — agla paper wahi se banta hai.
