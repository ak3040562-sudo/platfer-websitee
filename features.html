For teachers and coaching owners

Useful teaching technology should make your work simpler

For a small tuition class, a growing coaching centre or a teacher building an online audience, the best setup is the one that fits the real teaching routine. Platfer brings learning activities and everyday management into a single platform, helping teachers organise lessons and helping students access learning resources.

When choosing a teaching or coaching app, consider how it handles your class format, learner communication, study material, assessments and records. Start with the tasks that take the most time today, test the workflow with one batch, and expand only when it genuinely helps your students.

Before you get started
  • Choose the class or batch you want to organise first.
  • Prepare the first lesson and any supporting notes.
  • Tell students where to find lessons and updates.
  • Review feedback and adjust the workflow.
FAQs

Frequently asked questions about Platfer features

Quick answers to common questions from teachers, institutes and students.

What features does Platfer offer?

Platfer supports teaching and learning workflows such as live classes, study material, online tests, attendance, batch organisation, fee-related workflows and coaching discovery. Availability may depend on the feature and account setup.

Can teachers use Platfer for offline coaching?

Yes. Teachers can use the platform to support offline batches with digital resources, practice, attendance and other available management tools.

Can I teach online with Platfer?

Platfer is designed to help teachers bring lessons online with live teaching and supporting learning resources. Check the app for the current options available to your account.

How can students use Platfer?

Students can access learning resources and classes available to them, practise with tests, and explore coaching options where those services are available.

Does Platfer replace every school or coaching system?

No single tool suits every institution. Review the workflows you need, test them with a small batch, and confirm that they match your teaching and record-keeping requirements.

How do I get started?

Download Platfer, set up your account and begin with the teaching or learning workflow you need first.
