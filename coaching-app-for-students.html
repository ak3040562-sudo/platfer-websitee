Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can students find coaching on Platfer?
Yes. Students can discover coaching on Platfer by subject and city, look at what a coaching offers, and join the right batch with a batch code. Discovery works both ways: good teachers in small towns become visible to students who would never have walked past their door.
Can I plan my study?
Yes. The Study Planner lets you break your syllabus into chapters and topics, set when you will cover them, and get reminders at the times you choose. A plan that lives next to your classes and tests — not in a diary you forget — is far more likely to survive the month.
Can I ask questions?
Yes. You can post questions in Q&A and use teacher doubt support where your coaching offers it. Asking inside your batch means your own teacher sees the question in the context of what was taught — a far better answer than a random search result or a stranger’s video comment section.
Is the app free for students?
Joining a batch with a code and learning from your teacher is free to start. Full access plans start from around ₹99 a month for students who want everything unlocked.
Can a student be in batches of different teachers?
Yes. School tuition, a NEET Biology batch and an English batch from three different teachers all live in the same app, each in its own space.

Practical guide

What students need from a coaching app

Students need one place to discover coaching, join classes, access recordings and notes, practise with tests, plan study and ask questions. Platfer is designed to connect those learning activities rather than making students switch between unrelated apps.

Find coaching in your city

Search coaching by subject and city, see what each one actually offers, and join the batch that fits. Discovery replaces the old way — asking around and hoping — with a clear view of your real options before you pay anyone anything.

Join live and recorded classes

One app holds every subject’s classes: live when they happen, recorded when you revise. You stop depending on links forwarded in chats — your timetable, your teachers and your material are organised batch by batch.

Read notes and PDFs

Notes and PDFs shared by your teacher stay inside the batch, next to the class they belong to. Revision in exam week means opening the batch, not scrolling months of messages to find one file.
